# Football research that carries its evidence

**An independent prototype by Muhammad Mustafa.** Proposed for discussion with The Touchline; no affiliation or endorsement.

## The workflow

Choose a club, opponent and period → inspect a computed claim → review supporting results → customize a graphic → export PNG, caption and evidence JSON.

## What can be demonstrated today

- 11,664 recorded La Liga matches, September 1995–October 2025; final season partial.
- Three curated examples with selected primary-source checks.
- Scope-preserving square and landscape graphics.
- Club trends, scoring-environment comparisons and retrospective forecast evaluation.
- Source/evidence records, consistency checks and visible data limitations.
- A subscription-free historical browser demo; a separate server architecture for future licensed live ingestion.

## A small pilot to consider

Start with a discovery call. If the team sees value, agree on a small set of historical editorial tasks and brand requirements. Compare completion time and review errors against their existing workflow; collect feedback on whether the output is useful. Agree on acceptance criteria before setting a delivery date or price.

Possible outputs: a team-approved graphic template, reproducible research examples and a brief findings report. No audience-growth or revenue outcome is promised.

## Decisions before a commercial rollout

Resolve source/publication rights; confirm licensed live/event coverage if required; select hosting and monitoring; agree on ownership of the code and original creative work, support expectations and commercial scope. Do not transfer third-party data rights that have not been established.
